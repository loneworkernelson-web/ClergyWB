Privacy & Security

Your Privacy is Paramount.

This "Tōku Hauora" app is designed with your privacy as its core principle.

  • Client-Side Encryption: All data you enter (check-ins, notes, trends) is **encrypted** using your PIN *before* it is saved.
  • Local-First Storage: Your encrypted data is stored *exclusively* on your device within this web browser's private database (`IndexedDB`).
  • Zero Knowledge: Your unencrypted data is never sent to any server, and no one (not even the app's creator) can read it without your PIN. Your PIN is never stored directly.
  • Optional Cloud Sync: If you choose to sign in, your *already encrypted* data is synced to a private cloud database. No one, not even Google, can read this data.
  • You Control Your Data: You can export a decrypted backup or clear your encrypted data at any time using the options in the "Settings" section.

This app is a tool for personal reflection and awareness. It is not a diagnostic tool and does not replace professional medical or psychological advice.
