Privacy Information

Your Privacy is Paramount.

This "Tōku Hauora" app is built on a "Zero-Knowledge" principle. We cannot see your data, even if you use cloud sync.

  • Mandatory PIN: Your 4-digit PIN is now mandatory. It is used to generate a unique encryption key on your device.
  • Client-Side Encryption: All your data (check-ins, notes, etc.) is encrypted on your device using your key *before* it is saved.
  • Local-Only (Default): By default, your encrypted data is stored *exclusively* on your device in the browser. No one can access it.
  • Optional Cloud Sync: If you choose to sign in, your encrypted data is synced to a private cloud database. This allows you to restore your data or use multiple devices.
  • Zero-Knowledge: We only store encrypted "gibberish" in the cloud. We do not have your PIN or your encryption key, so we can never read your data.

Data Export & Deletion: You can export your (unencrypted) data or clear all stored data from this app via the Settings page.

This app is a tool for personal reflection and awareness. It is not a diagnostic tool and does not replace professional medical or psychological advice.

Welcome to Tōku Hauora!

This is your 100% private wellbeing hub. All your data stays on this device, secured by your PIN. No accounts, no cloud, no one sees it but you.

This app is a tool for reflection, not a replacement for professional advice.

The 5 Modules

You can interact with 5 main modules from the Home screen:

  • Check-ins (Warning/Positive): A weekly reflection on your overall wellbeing.
  • Sabbath Quality: A specific check-in on how restful your day off was.
  • My Rule of Life: Define your core intentions and track how you're living into them.
  • Gratitude Journal: A simple, daily space to note what you're grateful for.

See Your Progress

The "Trends" tab (bottom of screen) is where your data comes alive. You'll see:

  • Visual charts of your check-in and "Rule of Life" history.
  • Wellbeing Insights that automatically find patterns in your data over time.

The more you use the app, the more useful these insights will become.

Your Data, Your Control

Visit the "Settings" tab (bottom of screen) at any time to:

  • Change or Remove your PIN.
  • Export a full backup of all your data.
  • Import a backup file (e.g., on a new device).
  • Clear All Data permanently from this browser.
