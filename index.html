Privacy Information

Your Privacy is Paramount.

This "Taha Hinengaro Check" app is designed with your privacy as its core principle.

  • Local Data Storage: All data you enter (check-in responses, notes, trends) is stored *exclusively* on your device within this web browser's local storage. It is not sent to any server or third party.
  • No Account Needed: You do not need to create an account or log in to use this app.
  • Offline Functionality: The core features of the app work offline once the page is loaded.
  • You Control Your Data: You can view and clear your data at any time using the options in the "Settings" section.

App Lock: For enhanced privacy on your device, consider using your device's built-in screen lock features (PIN, fingerprint, Face ID). This web app itself does not provide an additional app lock.

Data Export & Deletion: You can copy your raw data or clear all stored data from this app via the Settings page.

This app is a tool for personal reflection and awareness. It is not a diagnostic tool and does not replace professional medical or psychological advice.
